# 图标说明

公开版图标为本项目通过图像生成工具新生成的纸张字标设计，没有使用第三方图片作为输入。

设计元素：暖白纸张、深墨绿色衬线字母 M、向下箭头与陶土色书签。原始文件为 `Assets/LightMDIcon.png`；构建脚本生成标准尺寸的 macOS 图标资源。

图标在项目方有权授权的范围内与项目一并按 MIT 许可证提供。生成式图像不意味着自动取得排他性著作权；本项目不对图标设计主张额外的独占使用限制。

## 生成提示词

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final raster macOS application icon for LightMD, a personal Markdown reader. Design a completely new, refined literary stationery icon, a quiet editorial/art-book feeling. Square 1024x1024 composition. One clean macOS-style rounded-square tile of warm ivory handmade paper with extremely subtle fine paper grain, gently layered paper sheets visible along the lower edge, and a small muted terracotta ribbon bookmark tucked into the upper right edge. In the center, one large elegant dark forest-green serif capital letter M, with a smaller simple downward arrow immediately to its right, together clearly reading as a restrained Markdown symbol. Exact visible text: M only, plus the downward arrow symbol, no other letters. Balanced geometric composition and generous negative space; the symbol should remain crisp and very legible at 32 and 64 pixels. Sophisticated muted palette: warm white/ivory, very dark green, tiny terracotta accent. The letter should have carefully crafted typography, like a tasteful book cover, not ornate calligraphy. Extremely restrained soft depth, matte paper, no glossy plastic, no clutter. The complete tile fits inside the canvas with a narrow transparent margin, with smoothly rounded clean outer edges and completely transparent outside corners. No characters, no people, no cartoon scene, no photography, no pen, no surrounding objects, no device mockup, no app screenshot, no brand references, no watermark or signature.

生成时要求透明背景；实际输出为 1254 × 1254 PNG，构建时再生成所需的图标尺寸。
