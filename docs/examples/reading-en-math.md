# Mathematics on the page

An inline expression such as $E=mc^2$ stays with its sentence. Longer equations get their own space, with the surrounding text still easy to follow.

## From line to display

A formula can be read as part of the prose or as a separate step in a derivation.

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)
$$

The space around a display equation lets its symbols remain clear.

$$
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx=\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1=\frac{1}{3}
$$

Continue reading without losing the thread of the argument.
