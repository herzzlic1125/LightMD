# A little room to read

Good typography gives words room to breathe. LightMD brings **English prose**, 中文正文 and mathematics together on one quiet page.

## Typography & rhythm · 字体与留白

阅读的舒适感，来自清晰的层级、适度的行距与自然的段落节奏。中文正文使用宋体，英文与数字 0123456789 使用系统衬线字体，让不同文字各有特点，又保持协调。

**重要的内容，用粗体强调。** *一段轻声的补充，用斜体表达。* 行内代码 `let reading = true` 保留清晰的等宽节奏。

A short paragraph leaves a pause between ideas. Longer lines wrap naturally within a measured reading width, keeping the page easy to follow.

## Mathematics, in context · 公式阅读

An inline expression such as $E=mc^2$ belongs in the sentence. A longer derivation gets space of its own.

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}}\exp\!\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)
$$

$$
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx=\left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1=\frac{1}{3}
$$

Read at your own pace. 让文字与公式，都回到阅读本身。
